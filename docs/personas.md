# User Personas

## Primary Personas (Customers)

### 1. Digital Agency Owner

**Name:** Minh (35, Ho Chi Minh City)

**Background:**
- Runs a 10-person digital agency
- Services: Website development, social media marketing, e-commerce
- Annual revenue: $200K
- Main challenge: Finding qualified leads

**Goals:**
- ✅ Build a predictable sales pipeline
- ✅ Increase deal closing rate from 10% → 30%
- ✅ Scale from 2-3 clients/month → 10+ clients/month
- ✅ Reduce time spent on lead research

**Pain Points:**
- ⏰ Spends 10+ hours/week on manual lead research
- 📉 Low conversion rate (cold outreach < 5%)
- 🎯 Hard to identify businesses ready to buy
- 💸 Can't afford dedicated sales team

**How Insight Hub Helps:**
- 🔍 Discovers 100+ qualified leads per week
- 📊 Provides lead scores + insights (e.g., "No website, strong reviews, high potential")
- 📧 Automates personalized email campaigns
- 💰 Increases conversion rate through better targeting

**Success Metric:** 3x more qualified meetings booked per month

## Key Insights

### Common Threads
- ✅ All personas struggle with **lead qualification**
- ✅ All need **contextual insights**, not just data
- ✅ All value **time savings** over raw features
- ✅ All want **measurable ROI**

### Product Implications
- 🎯 Lead scoring must be explainable
- 📊 Industry-specific insights are critical
- 📧 Automation saves time but must feel personal
- 💰 Pricing should align with value delivered
