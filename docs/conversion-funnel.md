# Conversion Funnel

## Overview

The conversion funnel maps how Insight Hub transforms **raw data** into **paying customers**.

**Goal:** Maximize value at each stage while minimizing friction.

---

## The Funnel Stages

```
Discovery → Enrichment → Insight → Scoring → Action → Conversion → Revenue
```

---

## Stage 1: Lead Discovery

### Input
- Location + industry criteria (e.g., "Coffee shops in Ho Chi Minh City")

### Process
- Google Maps extraction
- Data normalization
- Basic validation

### Output
- List of 100-1,000 raw business leads

### Metrics
- **Volume:** Leads extracted per search
- **Quality:** % with complete data (name, address, phone)
- **Target:** 90%+ data completeness

---

## Stage 2: Lead Enrichment

### Input
- Raw lead data

### Process
- Website analysis
- Email discovery
- Social media detection
- Tech stack identification

### Output
- Enriched lead profiles

### Metrics
- **Enrichment rate:** % of leads with website data
- **Coverage:** Average data points per lead
- **Target:** 70%+ have website or social data

---

## Stage 3: Insight Generation

### Input
- Enriched lead data

### Process
- Apply industry-specific rules
- Generate insights
- Contextualize with benchmarks

### Output
- Business insights + opportunities

### Metrics
- **Insights per lead:** Average 3-5 actionable insights
- **Accuracy:** % validated by customers
- **Target:** 80%+ accuracy

---

## Stage 4: Lead Scoring

### Input
- Insights

### Process
- Calculate opportunity score (0-100)
- Explain score breakdown
- Prioritize by urgency

### Output
- Scored and ranked leads

### Metrics
- **Score distribution:** 10% hot, 30% qualified, 40% good, 20% poor
- **Conversion correlation:** High scores → high conversion
- **Target:** 80%+ of 90+ scores convert to meetings

---

## Stage 5: Action (Outreach)

### Input
- Scored leads

### Process

#### Manual (Phase 1-2)
- Sales team reviews insights
- Crafts personalized pitch
- Sends email or makes call

#### Automated (Phase 3+)
- Insight-driven email templates
- Automated sending
- Follow-up sequences

### Output
- Outreach campaigns

### Metrics
- **Response rate:** % who reply
- **Meeting rate:** % who book meeting
- **Target:** 15%+ response, 10%+ meeting booking

---

## Stage 6: Conversion (Sales)

### Input
- Qualified meetings

### Process
- Discovery call
- Demo/presentation
- Proposal delivery
- Negotiation

### Output
- Closed deals

### Metrics
- **Win rate:** % of meetings → deals
- **Average deal size:** Revenue per closed deal
- **Sales cycle:** Days from meeting → close
- **Target:** 30%+ win rate, 30-day cycle

---

## Stage 7: Revenue & Retention

### Input
- Customers

### Process
- Service delivery
- Customer success
- Upsell/cross-sell

### Output
- Revenue + referrals

### Metrics
- **Customer LTV:** Lifetime value
- **Retention:** % still customers after 12 months
- **NPS:** Net Promoter Score
- **Target:** 80%+ retention, NPS 50+

---

## Full Funnel Metrics (Example)

### Scenario: Digital Agency using Insight Hub

```
1,000 leads extracted
   ↓ (70% enrichment rate)
700 leads enriched
   ↓ (60% have actionable insights)
420 qualified leads
   ↓ (10% are "hot" 90+ score)
42 hot leads
   ↓ (15% outreach response rate)
6 qualified meetings
   ↓ (30% win rate)
2 new customers
   ↓ ($3,500 average deal)
$7,000 revenue
```

### Conversion Rates
- **Lead → Qualified:** 42%
- **Qualified → Hot:** 10%
- **Hot → Meeting:** 15%
- **Meeting → Customer:** 30%
- **Overall (Lead → Customer):** 0.2%

### Economics
- **CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost):** Insight Hub fee + sales time
- **LTV (Lifetime Value):** $3,500 initial + $5K/year recurring
- **LTV/CAC Ratio:** Target 5:1

## Conversion Optimization Strategies

### Stage 1-2: Improve Lead Quality
- ✅ Better filtering (remove chains, outdated businesses)
- ✅ Focus on high-intent industries
- ✅ Enrich only high-potential leads

### Stage 3-4: Better Insights
- ✅ Industry-specific rules
- ✅ Dynamic weights based on customer feedback
- ✅ Benchmark data for context

### Stage 5: Personalized Outreach
- ✅ Reference specific insights ("I noticed you don't have online ordering...")
- ✅ Show ROI calculation ("You're losing $X per month")
- ✅ Timing (avoid weekends, holidays)

### Stage 6: Faster Sales
- ✅ Pre-built demos for common scenarios
- ✅ Insight-driven proposals
- ✅ Case studies from similar businesses

### Stage 7: Maximize LTV
- ✅ Deliver results quickly (prove ROI)
- ✅ Upsell complementary services
- ✅ Referral program (incentivize word-of-mouth)

---

## Automation Roadmap

### Phase 1: Manual (Months 1-3)
- Humans do everything
- Focus: Validate insights accuracy
- **Conversion:** 0.1-0.2%

### Phase 2: Semi-Automated (Months 4-6)
- Automated scoring + insights
- Manual outreach + sales
- **Conversion:** 0.3-0.5%

### Phase 3: Mostly Automated (Months 7-12)
- Automated insights + scoring + email
- Manual closing
- **Conversion:** 0.5-1.0%

### Phase 4: Full Automation (Year 2+)
- End-to-end automation
- Humans for complex deals only
- **Conversion:** 1.0-2.0%

## Channel-Specific Funnels

### Email Outreach
```
1,000 emails sent
   ↓ (30% open rate)
300 opens
   ↓ (10% reply rate)
30 replies
   ↓ (30% meeting rate)
10 meetings
   ↓ (30% close rate)
3 customers
```

### LinkedIn Outreach
```
100 connection requests
   ↓ (50% accept)
50 connections
   ↓ (20% respond to message)
10 conversations
   ↓ (50% meeting rate)
5 meetings
   ↓ (40% close rate)
2 customers
```

### Referral
```
10 customer referrals
   ↓ (80% meeting rate - warm intro)
8 meetings
   ↓ (60% close rate - trust transfer)
5 customers
```

**Insight:** Referrals convert 10x better than cold outreach!

## Funnel Health Indicators

### Green Flags ✅
- High response rate (>15%)
- Short sales cycle (<30 days)
- Low churn (<10% annually)
- High NPS (>50)

### Yellow Flags ⚠️
- Declining response rate
- Lengthening sales cycle
- Price objections increasing
- Customer complaints

### Red Flags 🚨
- Response rate <5%
- Win rate <20%
- High early churn
- NPS negative

- Response rate

## Funnel Experiments

### What to Test

1. **Insight Presentation**
   - A: "No website detected"
   - B: "Missing website costs you $500/month in lost customers"
   - **Hypothesis:** B has higher conversion (emotional + $)

2. **Outreach Timing**
   - A: Send Monday morning
   - B: Send Wednesday afternoon
   - **Hypothesis:** Wednesday has higher open rate

3. **Email Subject Lines**
   - A: "Quick question about [Business Name]"
   - B: "Found $5K revenue opportunity for [Business Name]"
   - **Hypothesis:** B has higher open rate but lower response (too salesy)

4. **Follow-up Cadence**
   - A: 3-day spacing
   - B: 7-day spacing
   - **Hypothesis:** 7 days reduces annoyance, maintains conversion
