# Problem Statement

## The Challenge

**Vietnamese SMEs are sitting on a goldmine of potential customers, but they don't know where to dig.**

---

## Market Context

### The Data Explosion
- 📱 **5M+ businesses** on Google Maps Vietnam
- 🌐 **70%+ businesses** have some online presence (Facebook, website, Zalo)
- ⭐ **Millions of reviews** contain buying signals

### The Intelligence Gap
Despite abundant data, businesses struggle with:

1. **Discovery Problem**
   - How do I find businesses that need my services?
   - Manual research takes hours per lead
   - Generic lead lists are low quality

2. **Context Problem**
   - Who should I contact first?
   - What's their pain point?
   - How do I personalize my pitch?

3. **Conversion Problem**
   - Which leads are worth pursuing?
   - What's the right message?
   - When should I follow up?

---

## Why Existing Solutions Fail

### ❌ Generic CRM Tools (HubSpot, Salesforce)
- Built for Western markets, don't understand Vietnamese SMEs
- No local data integration (Google Maps VN, Zalo, Vietnamese websites)
- Too expensive for small agencies

### ❌ Lead Generation Services
- Provide phone numbers without context
- No quality scoring or insights
- One-time lists, not a platform

### ❌ Manual Research
- Doesn't scale beyond 10-20 leads/week
- Inconsistent quality
- No systematic methodology

### ❌ Web Scraping Tools
- Just raw data extraction
- No intelligence layer
- No action guidance

---

## Pain Points by Persona

### Digital Agency Owner
> "I spend 2 hours researching each potential client. I can only reach out to 5 businesses per week. I don't know if they're even ready to buy."

**Pain Points:**
- ⏰ Manual research is time-consuming
- 🎯 Low response rates (< 5%)
- 💸 Can't scale sales pipeline

### Business Consultant
> "I know coffee shops need better systems, but I don't know WHICH ones. I waste time pitching to businesses that aren't interested."

**Pain Points:**
- 🔍 Hard to identify qualified leads
- 📊 No data to support recommendations
- 🚫 Generic pitches don't convert

### SME Owner (Secondary Persona)
> "I don't know what technology I need. Salespeople keep calling but nobody understands my business."

**Pain Points:**
- 🤷 Don't know what solutions exist
- 🎭 Generic sales pitches are annoying
- 💰 Can't justify ROI without proof
