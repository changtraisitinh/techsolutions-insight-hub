# F&B (Food & Beverage) Playbook

## Industry Overview

### Market Size (Vietnam)
- **500K+ F&B establishments**
- Categories: Restaurants, cafes, bakeries, street food, catering
- Growth: 10-15% annually
- Digital adoption: 40% have basic online presence

### Key Challenges
1. **Low margins** (5-15% net profit)
2. **High competition** (10+ competitors within 1km)
3. **Labor intensive** (staff costs 30-40% of revenue)
4. **Inventory waste** (food spoilage)
5. **Limited digital skills**

### Digital Transformation Priorities
1. 🔥 **Online ordering** (highest ROI)
2. 🔥 **Delivery integration** (GrabFood, ShopeeFood)
3. ⭐ **Digital menu** (QR codes, tablets)
4. ⭐ **POS system** (inventory, sales tracking)
5. ✅ **Customer loyalty** (repeat business)

## F&B-Specific Insights

### High-Priority Gaps

#### 1. No Online Ordering
**Insight:** "Business has no online ordering system"

**Impact:**
- Missing 30-40% revenue opportunity
- Customers prefer to order via app
- Competitors with ordering win market share

**Recommended Solution:**
- Online ordering platform integration
- GrabFood + ShopeeFood onboarding
- QR code for table ordering

**Pitch Angle:**
> "Your competitors with online ordering are capturing 30% more revenue. Let's get you set up this week."

**Deal Size:** $2,000-5,000 (implementation + monthly fee)

---

#### 2. No Digital Menu
**Insight:** "Still using paper menus"

**Impact:**
- Hard to update prices
- Printing costs
- Unhygienic (post-COVID concern)

**Recommended Solution:**
- QR code digital menu
- Tablet menu for dine-in
- Integration with POS

**Pitch Angle:**
> "Customers now expect QR menus. Save $200/month on printing and update prices instantly."

**Deal Size:** $800-1,500

---

#### 3. No Delivery Integration
**Insight:** "Not listed on GrabFood or ShopeeFood"

**Impact:**
- Missing 40% of F&B orders (delivery trend)
- Lose customers who search on delivery apps

**Recommended Solution:**
- GrabFood merchant setup
- ShopeeFood onboarding
- Menu optimization for delivery

**Pitch Angle:**
> "60% of customers search for food on Grab. If you're not there, you don't exist to them."

**Deal Size:** $1,000-2,000 (setup + optimization)

---

#### 4. Poor Website or No Website
**Insight:** "No website or outdated website"

**Impact:**
- Hard to find online
- Can't show menu to customers
- Miss SEO opportunity

**Recommended Solution:**
- Restaurant website with menu
- Google Business optimization
- Instagram integration

**Pitch Angle:**
> "When customers Google '[cuisine] near me,' your competitors show up. You should too."

**Deal Size:** $1,500-3,000
